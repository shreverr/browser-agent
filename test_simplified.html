Types of Cooking Oil

Zepto offers various kinds of cooking oils, such as:

Vegetable oil

Vegetable oils are among the most common, known for their neutral flavours and versatility in cooking, frying, and baking.

Groundnut oil

These are often selected for their distinct flavours and health benefits. Groundnut (peanut) oil is popular for frying due to its high smoke point.

Coconut oil

Coconut oil is known for its delicious and distinct taste, making it an essential in many South Indian dishes.

Olive oil

Olive oil is prized for its unique nutritional profiles and being one of the healthiest oils to cook with or use as dressing in salads.

Avocado oil

Avocado oil, made from the seed of avocadoes , is another healthy option as it is rich in monounsaturated fats and is suitable for high-heat cooking.

Sesame oil

Gingelly (sesame) is commonly used in South Indian cooking for its distinct, robust flavour.

Organic oils

Organic oils are produced without synthetic pesticides or chemicals, appealing to consumers who prioritise natural and sustainable food sources.

Blended oils

These oils combine various oils to balance health benefits, flavour, and cooking properties.
